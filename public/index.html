Who I Am

About Me

I'm a self-taught full-stack web developer and SEO specialist based in Keffi, Nasarawa State, Nigeria — operating under OneXportal. Over the past 3+ years I've designed, built, and deployed websites for businesses, creatives, and entrepreneurs across different industries.

I work with modern AI development tools to move faster — but I remain fully responsible for every technical decision: the architecture, database design, security layers, API integrations, deployment, and long-term maintainability of everything I ship. AI accelerates my implementation. The engineering judgment is mine.

AI-Assisted Full-Stack Development

I use AI as a development accelerator — not as a replacement for engineering judgment. AI helps me move faster with research, implementation, debugging and documentation. I still own the architecture, frontend, backend, APIs, authentication, security, responsive design, validation, deployment and maintenance of every project.

The goal isn't to generate code quickly. The goal is to deliver a website that works reliably, looks professional, performs across devices, can be maintained after launch, and solves your actual business problem. I also evaluate AI output critically — reviewing generated code for security gaps, logic errors, and maintainability issues before anything ships. You don't need to take my word for it — evaluate me by what I've actually built, deployed, and can demonstrate.
